How score is calculated

Every game has exactly one durak, so at a table of n players your share of the blame is 1/n — half at a two-player table, a third at three, a quarter at four. Add that up across your games and you have how often you should have been the durak. Score is the gap between that and how often you actually were:

score = (expected durak rate − actual durak rate) × 100

Say you play 5 games: 1 with two players, 3 with three, 1 with four. That expects (1 × ½ + 3 × ⅓ + 1 × ¼) ÷ 5 = 35%. If you have a durak rate of 30%, you score +5; If you have a durak rate of 40% you score −5.
